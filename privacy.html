Privacy

What we keep, why, and what we never touch. This is a plain-language summary, not formal legal terms.

What we never ask for

Your wallet keys or recovery phrase. Everything you pay is signed inside your own wallet.

In the Telegram bot

  • When you run /call, the claim you typed and the draft the AI wrote are saved for up to 30 days, under a short random code, so the vote buttons keep working.
  • Votes are stored as scrambled codes, not as Telegram IDs or names.
  • The text of your claim is sent to Google's Gemini model to write the draft.

On the website

  • Market data is requested through our server, which holds the Panta key so your browser never sees it.
  • When you create or trade, your wallet address and the transaction signature go to Panta, and a trade can be reported to Panta so it can be credited to this app.
  • Our host (Vercel) keeps standard request logs. We do not run analytics or advertising scripts.

Questions or removal

Open an issue on GitHub and we will deal with it.
