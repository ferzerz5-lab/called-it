Honest status

What we have checked, what we have not, and where the limits are. Updated October 2026.

Checked against the live Panta API

  • Market discovery, including each market's phase
  • Real buy quotes and a built buy transaction, while a buyable market was open
  • The create-market quote: $50.00, made of a $40 platform fee and $10 starting liquidity
  • Market image upload
  • A built create-market transaction (a dry run, so nothing was signed)
  • The Telegram bot running around the clock, with votes kept in a database

Not checked yet

  • A complete signed trade or market creation on mainnet. Signing needs a funded wallet.
  • Wallet warnings. A review of this site has been requested from Phantom, and a new site can show a warning until it is reviewed.

Known limits

  • Panta's API only supports buying in a market's primary phase. Secondary-phase markets are labelled and linked to panta.market.
  • Sometimes no market is open to buy. That is why creating one, with a group vote first, matters.
  • The group leaderboard is not built yet.
  • Drafts and votes are kept for 30 days, then expire.
  • The AI can make mistakes. Read the rule and the sources before you create anything.
