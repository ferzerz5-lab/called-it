Questions people ask

Do I need a wallet just to look around?

No. Browsing markets, searching with /odds and drafting with /call need nothing. You only need a Solana wallet such as Phantom when you create a market or buy.

Does I-Called-It ever hold my money?

Never. Every payment is signed in your own wallet, and we never see your keys or recovery phrase. The app only builds the transaction and asks your wallet to approve it.

Why does creating a market cost about $50?

That is Panta's fee, not ours: $40 platform fee plus $10 of starting liquidity. The exact amount is shown before you sign. It is also why each draft has a group vote, so nobody pays for a market nobody wants.

Why did the bot refuse my claim?

A market has to be settled by a public website, so opinions, jokes, private bets and anything about harming someone are turned down. The bot explains why and suggests a version that can work.

Is the market data live?

Yes, it comes straight from Panta. Some markets are in a secondary phase that Panta's API cannot buy into, so we label them and link to panta.market. At times there is nothing open to buy at all.

Can I really trade right now?

The buy flow is built and works up to the wallet signature. A complete signed trade has not been verified end to end yet, because that needs a funded wallet, and your wallet may warn about a new site until it is reviewed. The Honest status page lists exactly what is checked.

How does the group vote work?

Each person taps once to say they would trade it, and taps again to take the vote back. In a group, the Create button unlocks after three people agree. In a private chat, one tap is enough.

Who decides if a market is YES or NO?

Panta resolves it from the public sources named when the market was created. That is why the bot writes a precise rule and picks sources anyone can check.
