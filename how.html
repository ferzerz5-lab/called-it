How it works

From a group-chat argument to a settled market, in plain words.

1. Find or draft a market

/odds searches the live Panta markets. /call turns a claim into a YES/NO question with a deadline, a rule, and public sources.

2. Trade

Pick YES or NO and an amount. The page asks Panta for a quote, builds the transaction, and your wallet signs it.

3. Resolution

Panta resolves markets from the public sources named at creation, with a short dispute window before payout.

4. Claim

If you backed the right side, you claim your payout from the market.

Good to know

  1. You need a Solana wallet such as Phantom, with USDC to trade and a little SOL for network fees.
  2. Quotes expire after about 90 seconds. If one does, just get a new quote.
  3. Creating a new market costs a USDC fee set by Panta, shown before you confirm.
  4. Buy-only for now: positions are bought at the market price and settle when the market resolves.
  5. Not every claim can be a market. It needs a public website that can prove the answer.

I-Called-It is an independent project built on the Panta API. It is not financial advice.
