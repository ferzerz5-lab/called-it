Risk

In plain words, before you put money into anything here.

You can lose what you put in

A prediction market pays out only if you are right. If you are wrong, you lose your stake. Only use money you can afford to lose.

Rules can be misread

Markets are settled from public websites. Sources can be wrong, change, or be read differently than you expected. The AI writes the rule and picks the sources, and it can make mistakes, so read both before you sign.

Transactions are final

Once your wallet signs and sends a payment, it cannot be undone by us. Check the amount shown before you approve.

Check your local rules

Prediction markets are not allowed or legal everywhere. It is your job to check what applies to you.

Not financial advice

Odds show what other people are willing to pay. They are not a promise or a recommendation. I-Called-It is an independent project built on the Panta API.
